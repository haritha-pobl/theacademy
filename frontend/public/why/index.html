Why This Name

We named ourselves after what happens next — not what we teach.

Most institutes name themselves after a subject. We named ourselves after a moment: the one where you decide to move — toward a first job, a sharper skill set, or a company of your own. "The Academy" is deliberately simple. "Your Next Chapter" is the promise underneath it.

Who We Are

Built and backed by POBL — a real ecosystem, not a classroom in isolation.

POBL has spent years connecting Indian founders with the right mentors, professionals, and capital at every stage of their business. The Academy is the education layer of that same ecosystem — which means you're not just enrolled in a program, you have a line into a working network of operators and hiring partners.

Why We're Building This

Skills go stale in months now. Most training still teaches years-old playbooks.

AI has rewritten how marketing, HR, and finance work in the last eighteen months alone — but many women are also navigating a second, quieter barrier: the practical logistics of actually showing up, whether that's a career break, a lack of industry exposure, or simply no ecosystem beyond family to lean on. We built The Academy to answer both at once — real, current business skills, and a place designed to make attending genuinely possible.

"This isn't a support program. It's a serious business education — that happens to understand what your life actually looks like."
